# Lost device

1. Verify account ownership through the normal account session or support review.
2. Ask the available installation to request a fresh challenge and submit a
   valid signed deactivation; do not accept a copied ticket as authority.
3. If the device is unavailable, use the controlled support reset procedure and
   record actor, reason, evidence class, and affected activation only.
4. Confirm the slot is free for the replacement. Do not reveal credentials,
   public-key fingerprints, or unrelated activation data.
